# Canvas status and reading order

These canvases preserve design history, concept sketches and benchmark/fixture inputs. Names such as “Current,” “Working,” “Confirmed” or “Approved” describe their original snapshots; they do not establish current implementation, acceptance or permission. Keep the archived bytes and [provenance manifest](manifest.json) unchanged.

Use this precedence when reading them:

1. The [current roadmap](../roadmap/README.md) and [research evidence vocabulary](../research/README.md#evidence-vocabulary) distinguish existing facilities from proposed work.
2. The [repository README](../../README.md), [source contracts](../../src/ports.d.ts) and [owner/orchestrator boundary](../ORCHESTRATOR-BOUNDARY.md) describe current surfaces: Codex subscription execution, SQLite state, controller-owned candidates and policy-authorized delivery. The owner still interacts through conversation.
3. The [later v0.1 closure record](../releases/0.1/baseline-closure.json) governs that program's outcome and hold, ahead of earlier readiness notes, release progress text and benchmark/hill-climbing checklists.
4. The [architecture design and original canvas](../ARCHITECTURE.md), workflow/whiteglove/server proposals and vision/status concepts explain intent. They do not establish Pi/Docker execution, merge-queue support, a notification delivery ledger, a standalone orchestrator or 24/7 monitoring in the current implementation.
5. Benchmark briefs and the disposable StickyCanvas integration fixture preserve inputs and examples. A fixture, concept screenshot or component test is not actual application acceptance.

This legend also applies to the original canvas copies under `docs/` and `docs/benchmarks/` and historical copies in release evidence. Read stronger runtime/acceptance claims only with their exact source or observed receipt. No canvas, passed controller CI, restored quota or elapsed time lifts an execution hold, reopens the closed original baseline or replaces separately authorized independent grading.
