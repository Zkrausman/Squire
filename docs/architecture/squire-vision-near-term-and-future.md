# Squire delivery vision (editable diagram)

Open [`squire-vision-near-term-and-future.tldraw`](squire-vision-near-term-and-future.tldraw) in the offline tldraw app. This owner-reviewed diagram was approved for committing on 2026-09-26. It is an architecture **plan**, not proof that future capabilities ship today.

The canvas distinguishes:

1. **Snapshot as of 2026-09-26:** pinned Squire v0.2 and a Windows-only read-only active-run view; Gelt ZAR-219 and Squire AIDEV-339 generated `UNVERIFIED` candidates requiring external review and tests. This snapshot will age.
2. **Near-term target after [AIDEV-338](https://linear.app/geltagentictrading/issue/AIDEV-338/epic-focused-read-only-squire-run-dashboard-windows-local-web) and [AIDEV-341](https://linear.app/geltagentictrading/issue/AIDEV-341/squire-pilot-post-merge-dual-retros-with-gated-wiki-only-merge-and):** focused read-only dashboard, safe ticket-level preflight and reviewed/verified delivery; two post-merge read-only retros; separately scoped wiki-only PRs that the orchestrator may merge autonomously **only after** factual/privacy review and applicable exact-head gates; deduplicated, value-gated ticket pass. These are conditional goals, not shipped acceptance claims. AIDEV-340 depends on AIDEV-339, and AIDEV-341 depends on AIDEV-340.
3. **Eventual north star (provisional):** owner brings a broad idea; Squire researches and bounds it, decomposes it into valuable dependent tickets, orchestrates isolated implementation and independent review, and learns from verified outcomes. The owner expects little routine intervention, but live trades, credentials, deployment/new risk and ambiguous safety gates still have explicit authority stops. The north-star portion is expected to evolve.

A dashboard is a read-only projection, never approval or execution authority. Unverified candidates and unrun tests never become pass receipts. The diagram is documentation only; it does not install a runner, activate a workflow, or grant a blanket code-merge or trading permission.
