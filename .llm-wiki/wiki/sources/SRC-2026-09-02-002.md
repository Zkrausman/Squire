# AIDEV-216 Review Attempt 6 Findings

## Summary

Independent Review attempt 6 confirmed durable recovery for provisional `termination_failed` allocations, then found the equivalent registered-session gap. Atomic first-session registration and resumed live persistence clear the provisional allocation. If the now-registered process survives bounded termination, its exact generation and process identity remain durable on `SessionRegistration`, but restart cleanup previously inspected only `processAllocations` and returned without action.

R6-001 requires explicit cleanup to treat a current registered `live` or `launching` generation with exact `processIdentity` as actionable ownership. Restart may resolve that identity through the injected supervisor seam and repeat bounded termination. Unknown or mismatched identity remains blocked; only observed exit may mark that exact generation failed/exited. Same-role relaunch stays forbidden until this proof is persisted, after which resume uses the exact registered JSONL and increments generation.

## Key Takeaways

- Clearing provisional allocation ownership does not erase durable process ownership when the exact live session generation is registered.
- Restart cleanup must inspect both provisional allocations and registered live/launching identities.
- Generation and identity checks prevent stale cleanup from clobbering a newer process while repeated non-cooperative termination remains fail-closed.
