# AIDEV-216 Review Attempt 5 Findings

## Summary

Independent Review attempt 5 confirmed that normal process termination closes all tested post-settlement lease-loss windows, then exposed a non-cooperative-process gap. A process synchronously returned before registration could survive bounded SIGTERM and SIGKILL while its runner handle was discarded and its persisted `spawning` allocation lacked the process identity needed to target later cleanup.

R5-001 requires two monotonic forms of ownership. The runner must retain the exact process handle from creation until observed exit, and persistence must retain the exact owner, fencing token, generation, and process identity in a fail-closed `termination_failed` state. Retry or restart may resolve that identity through an injected supervisor seam and retry termination; only observed exit authorizes existing compensation. Unknown identity status remains blocked rather than being interpreted as exit.

## Key Takeaways

- Bounded kill attempts are not evidence that a process exited.
- A synchronously exposed process needs immediate allocating-handle tracking, before RPC client creation or registration.
- Durable exact-token retention enables explicit cross-restart reaping without authorizing replacement work or implementing AIDEV-224 startup reconciliation.
