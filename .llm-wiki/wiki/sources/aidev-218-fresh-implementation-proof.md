# AIDEV-218 Fresh Implementation Proof

A fresh implementation handoff can establish current acceptance independently when the exact delivery diff from the frozen base, clean output head/tree, contract validation, adversarial recovery tests, and ownership boundaries are recorded together. An unavailable historical session remains an immutable audit incident and must not be reconstructed or silently treated as current evidence. [[sources/aidev-218-plan-phase-boundary]] and [[sources/persisted-acceptance-authority-chain]] remain the applicable Plan and acceptance authorities.
